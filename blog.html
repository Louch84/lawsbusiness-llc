Distressed Properties: The Hidden Goldmine in Philly

February 17, 2026

Distressed properties - foreclosed, short sales, and bank-owned homes - are one of the best ways to get deals in Philadelphia. These properties often sell 20-50% below market value.

Types of distressed properties:

  • Foreclosures - Bank-owned properties after owner defaults
  • Short Sales - Lender agrees to sell for less than owed
  • Probate - Inherited properties from estates
  • Tax Liens - Properties with unpaid taxes

Whether you're buying for yourself or investing, distressed properties in Philly can be serious money. Check out Foreclosure.com below to find deals in your area.

Wholesale Real Estate: Make Money Without Buying

February 17, 2026

Wholesaling is one of the best ways to get into real estate with little to no money down. Here's how it works:

  1. Find a distressed property under market value
  2. Sign a contract with the seller
  3. Find a buyer (investor) who wants the deal
  4. Assign the contract to the buyer for a fee

You never actually buy the property - you just control it and flip the contract. Wholesalers in Philly typically make $5,000-$15,000 per deal.

The key: Build your buyers list first. Connect with real estate investors, flippers, and landlords who are ready to buy.
